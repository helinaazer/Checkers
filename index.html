How to Play

Goal

Capture all of your opponent's pieces, or leave them with no legal move on their turn.

Setup

Each side starts with 8 pieces (instead of the usual 12), filling the two rows closest to that player on the dark squares only. Black moves first.

Moving

Regular pieces move diagonally forward, one square at a time, onto an empty dark square.

Capturing

Jump diagonally over an adjacent opponent piece into the empty square just beyond it to capture it. If any capture is available to you, you must take one — you can't make a non-capturing move instead. If that same piece can capture again right after landing, it must keep jumping in the same turn.

Kings

A piece that reaches the far row becomes a king and can then move and capture diagonally in any direction, not just forward.

Reinforcements (this variant's twist)

Each side's own back row starts with only 4 of its 8 pieces. The first time that row is completely empty of your pieces, 4 fresh pieces appear there automatically — once per side, per game.

Winning

You win the moment your opponent has no pieces left, or no legal move on their turn.

Playing vs. the Computer

Pick a color on the home screen — the board is always oriented with your pieces at the bottom. The computer pauses to "think," then plays its move, including any forced multi-jumps, automatically.
